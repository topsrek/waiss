WAISS (Wiener AI Software Stammtisch) is a cozy get-together, planned monthly, for everyone who builds, codes & experiments with AI.

We'll kick things off with a short AI recap (~20 min) of what mattered last month. Then a few short talks: tool tips or show & tell of your own projects. Got something to show? Just let us know! After that it's an open round to chat, discuss and meet people.

Tuesday, 10 Nov 2026, 18:00
Café Ritter, Mariahilfer Str. 73, 1060 Vienna

Free entry, food and drinks at your own expense.

WAISS is organized by Telos Circle.
